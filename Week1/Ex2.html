About me

My name is Kim Hye-jun. I am a South Korean actress. I was born on May 8, 1995, in South Korea.I have always been passionate about acting and enjoy bringing different characters and stories to life.

My Acting Career

I began my acting career in 2015. Since then, I have had the opportunity to work on a variety of television dramas and films. Through each project, I have learned more about acting and developed my skills as an actress.

One of the projects that helped me gain wider recognition was Kingdom, where I played an important role. I have also continued to challenge myself by taking on different characters in various genres.
