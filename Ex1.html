Exploring Khmer Culture

The Khmer culture, originating from Cambodia, is rich in history and traditional.

Key Aspects of Khmer Culture

  • Traditional Cuisine
  • Festivals and Celebrations
  • Art and Architeture
    1. . Angkor Wat - A magnificent temple complex and a symbol of Khmer architectural brilliance.
    2. . Khmer New Year - A major festival marketing the start of the new year with traditional dances and ceremonies.
    3. . Tranditional Dance - Includes classical performances such as the Apsara dance, which is deeply rooted in Khmer trandition.
